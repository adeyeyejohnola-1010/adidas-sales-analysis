# adidas-sales-analysis
Adidas sales analysis using Excel to explore revenue, profitability, regional performance and sales trends through interactive dashboards
Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Adidas sales data from January 2020 to December 2021 using Microsoft Excel. The aim was to understand sales performance, evaluate profitability and identify the factors contributing to revenue across different regions, retailers, product categories and sales channels.

I used Excel to explore the dataset, calculate key performance metrics, identify business trends and develop an interactive dashboard to present the findings.

## Business Problem

Understanding total revenue alone does not provide enough information about business performance. A business also needs to understand where its revenue comes from, which products perform best, which retailers contribute the most and how profitable its sales activities are.

The goal of this analysis was to examine Adidas sales performance and provide insights that could support better business decisions relating to product performance, geographical distribution, retailer contributions and sales channels.


## Data Preparation and preprocessing

Before beginning the analysis, I examined the dataset to understand its structure and check its quality.

The preparation process included:

- Reviewing column names and data types.
- Checking for missing values and duplicate records.
- Ensuring the invoice date was properly formatted.
- Formatting sales and operating profit as currency.
- Formatting operating margin as a percentage.
- Organising the data for analysis and visualisation.

## Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Identify the highest-performing geographical regions.
- Analyse sales contributions across retailers.
- Examine product category performance.
- Compare the performance of different sales channels.
- Identify the top revenue-generating states and cities.
- Examine quarterly sales trends.
- Evaluate operating profit and operating margin

## Analysis and Business Questions

### 1. Regional Performance
**Business Question:** Which geographical regions contribute the most to Adidas' total revenue?

**Approach:** Grouped total sales by region to compare revenue contributions.

### 2. Retailer Performance
**Business Question:** Which retailers generate the highest sales revenue?

**Approach:** Compared total sales across retailers to identify their contributions to overall revenue.

### 3. Product Category Performance
**Business Question:** Which product categories generate the highest revenue?

**Approach:** Examined total sales across the available product categories.

### 4. Sales Channel Performance
**Business Question:** Which sales method contributes the most to total revenue?

**Approach:** Compared revenue generated through In-store, Outlet and Online channels.

### 5. Geographical Performance
**Business Question:** Which states and cities generate the highest sales?

**Approach:** Ranked states and cities based on total sales to identify the strongest geographical markets.

### 6. Sales Trend Analysis
**Business Question:** How does Adidas' sales performance change over time?

**Approach:** Analysed revenue across quarters to identify changes and patterns in sales performance.

### 7. Profitability Analysis
**Business Question:** How does operating profitability differ across product categories and sales channels?

**Approach:** Examined operating profit and operating margin to evaluate profitability beyond revenue.

# Key insights

- **Overall Performance:** Adidas generated approximately $899.90 million in total sales, with $332.13 million in operating profit and 2.48 million units sold.

- **Regional Performance:** The West region recorded the highest sales, contributing approximately $269.94 million.

- **Retailer Performance:** West Gear generated the highest retailer revenue at approximately $242.96 million, followed by Foot Locker.

- **Product Performance:** Men's Street Footwear was the leading product category, generating approximately $208.83 million in revenue.

- **Sales Channel Performance:** In-store sales generated the highest revenue at approximately $356.64 million.

- **State Performance:** New York recorded the highest state revenue at approximately $64.23 million.

- **City Performance:** Charleston recorded the highest city revenue at approximately $39.97 million.

- **Quarterly Performance:** The third quarter recorded the highest revenue at approximately $265.31 million.

- **Profitability:** The overall operating margin was approximately 36.91%.

## Recommendations

Based on my findings, I recommend the following:

1. **Regional Strategy:** Reviewing sales and distribution opportunities in lower-performing regions while maintaining strong performance in leading markets.

2. **Retailer Strategy:** Strengthen relationships with high-performing retail partners and investigate opportunities to improve sales among lower-performing retailers.

3. **Product Strategy:** Maintain adequate stock of high-performing product categories while reviewing demand for categories with lower sales contributions.

4. **Sales Channel Strategy:** Continue evaluating the In-store, Outlet and Online channels to identify opportunities for improved revenue and profitability.

5. **Geographical Strategy:** Using state and city performance to support inventory allocation and location-based sales planning.

6. **Profitability Strategy:** Monitor operating margins across products and sales channels to ensure that revenue generation is supported by healthy profitability.

## Conclusion

This project helped me explore how sales data can be used to understand business performance beyond total revenue.

By examining regional performance, retailer contributions, product categories, sales channels and profitability, I was able to identify the areas contributing most to Adidas' sales.
