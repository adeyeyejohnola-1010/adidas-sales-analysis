# adidas-sales-analysis
Adidas sales analysis using Excel to explore revenue, profitability, regional performance and sales trends through interactive dashboards
Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing Adidas sales data from January 2020 to December 2021 using Microsoft Excel. The aim was to understand sales performance, evaluate profitability and identify the factors contributing to revenue across different regions, retailers, product categories and sales channels.

I used Excel to explore the dataset, calculate key performance metrics, identify business trends and develop an interactive dashboard to present the findings.

##Business Problem

Understanding total revenue alone does not provide enough information about business performance. A business also needs to understand where its revenue comes from, which products perform best, which retailers contribute the most and how profitable its sales activities are.

The goal of this analysis was to examine Adidas sales performance and provide insights that could support better business decisions relating to product performance, geographical distribution, retailer contributions and sales channels.
## Project Objectives

- Evaluate overall sales and profitability.
- Identify the highest-performing geographical regions.
- Analyse sales contributions across retailers.
- Examine product category performance.
- Compare the performance of different sales channels.
- Identify the top revenue-generating states and cities.
- Examine quarterly sales trends.
- Evaluate operating profit and operating margin
- Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, analysis, calculations, PivotTables, charts and interactive dashboard development.

## Data Preparation and preprocessing

Before beginning the analysis, I examined the dataset to understand its structure and check its quality.

The preparation process included:

- Reviewing column names and data types.
- Checking for missing values and duplicate records.
- Ensuring the invoice date was properly formatted.
- Formatting sales and operating profit as currency.
- Formatting operating margin as a percentage.
- Organising the data for analysis and visualisation.
